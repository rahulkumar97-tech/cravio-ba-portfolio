# Cravio – Online Food Delivery Platform

## Project Overview

**Cravio** is a Business Analyst portfolio project based on an online food delivery platform. The project demonstrates how a Business Analyst analyzes a business problem, gathers requirements, documents functional and non-functional requirements, creates process and system diagrams, manages work in Jira, and prepares the project for testing and User Acceptance Testing (UAT).

The project follows a practical **Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)** and demonstrates the end-to-end BA workflow from requirement gathering to UAT readiness.

---

## Business Problem

Customers need a convenient way to discover restaurants, browse food items, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries from a single platform.

Restaurants need a platform to manage their profiles, menus, availability, and customer orders.

Delivery partners need a system to receive delivery assignments, update delivery status, and complete deliveries.

The platform also requires administrative capabilities for managing users, restaurants, orders, delivery partners, complaints, and platform-level reports.

---

## Project Objectives

* Understand and document business requirements.
* Convert business requirements into functional requirements.
* Prepare professional BA documentation.
* Visualize business processes and system interactions.
* Create user stories and acceptance criteria.
* Manage requirements and work items using Jira.
* Understand Agile/Scrum workflow.
* Prepare test scenarios and test cases.
* Understand UAT and the BA role during acceptance testing.

| Customer         | Browse food, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries                |
| Restaurant       | Manage restaurant profile, menu, availability, and orders                     |
| Delivery Partner | Accept deliveries, update status, navigate, and confirm delivery              |
| Admin            | Manage users, restaurants, orders, delivery partners, complaints, and reports |
| Payment Gateway  | Process and confirm online payments                                           |
| Business Analyst | Gather, analyze, document, and validate requirements                          |
| Development Team | Build the system based on approved requirements                               |

| Business Users   | Validate the solution during UAT                                              |

The project includes the following Business Analyst deliverables:

* Business Problem Definition
* Stakeholder Identification
* Requirement Gathering
* Meeting Minutes (MoM)
* Business Requirements Document (BRD)
* Functional Requirements Document (FRD)
* Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
* Customer Journey Wireframes
* UML Use Case Diagram
* Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD)
* Business Process Model and Notation (BPMN)
* User Stories
* Acceptance Criteria
* Jira Epics, Stories, and Tasks
* Test Scenarios and Test Cases
* UAT Planning
