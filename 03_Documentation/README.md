# Cravio – Project Documentation

This folder contains the formal Business Analyst documentation prepared for the **Cravio – Online Food Delivery Platform**.

These documents capture the business requirements, functional requirements, system requirements, and stakeholder discussions throughout the project lifecycle.

---

## Documentation Artifacts

| Document                                  | Purpose                                                                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Requirement Gathering                     | Captures the initial business needs and stakeholder requirements                                                        |
| MoM – Initial Client Requirement Meeting  | Records meeting discussions, decisions, and action items                                                                |
| BRD – Business Requirements Document      | Defines business objectives, scope, stakeholders, and high-level requirements                                           |
| FRD – Functional Requirements Document    | Defines detailed system functionality and expected behavior                                                             |
| SRS – Software Requirements Specification | Provides detailed software requirements, business rules, validations, NFRs, assumptions, dependencies, and traceability |

---

## Documentation Flow

```text
Requirement Gathering
        ↓
Meeting Minutes (MoM)
        ↓
Business Requirements Document (BRD)
        ↓
Functional Requirements Document (FRD)
        ↓
Software Requirements Specification (SRS)
```

---

## Business Analyst Responsibilities Demonstrated

Through these documents, the project demonstrates the following BA activities:

* Understanding the business problem
* Identifying stakeholders
* Gathering and analyzing requirements
* Documenting business requirements
* Defining functional requirements
* Identifying non-functional requirements
* Documenting business rules and validations
* Managing assumptions, constraints, and dependencies
* Maintaining requirement traceability
* Communicating requirements clearly for development and testing
