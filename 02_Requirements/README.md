# Cravio – Requirements

This section contains the requirement analysis and business requirement artifacts prepared for the **Cravio – Online Food Delivery Platform**.

The requirements were analyzed from the perspective of different stakeholders and converted into structured Business Analyst documentation.

---

## Requirement Analysis Approach

The requirement analysis process followed these steps:

1. Identify the business problem.
2. Identify key stakeholders.
3. Conduct initial requirement gathering.
4. Document stakeholder needs and expectations.
5. Convert business needs into business requirements.
6. Define functional and non-functional requirements.
7. Validate requirements for completeness and clarity.
8. Maintain traceability between requirements and project work items.

## Requirement Documents

The detailed requirements are documented through the following BA artifacts:

| Document              | Purpose                                                                                        |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Requirement Gathering | Captures initial stakeholder requirements                                                      |
| MoM                   | Records discussions, decisions, and action items from the client meeting                       |
| BRD                   | Defines business needs, objectives, scope, and high-level requirements                         |
| FRD                   | Defines detailed functional requirements and system behavior                                   |
| SRS                   | Provides structured software requirements, business rules, validations, NFRs, and traceability |

---

## Requirement Traceability

Requirements are connected with downstream project artifacts such as:

```text
Business Requirement
        ↓
Functional Requirement
        ↓
User Story
        ↓
Acceptance Criteria
        ↓
Jira Work Item
        ↓
Test Case
        ↓
UAT
```

This approach helps maintain consistency between the original business need and the final validation of the solution.
