# Cravio – Online Food Delivery Platform

A **Business Analyst portfolio case study** demonstrating the complete BA lifecycle for an online food delivery platform — from requirement analysis and documentation to Agile project management, testing, UAT, and traceability.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

**Cravio** is a self-created online food delivery platform case study designed to demonstrate practical **Business Analysis skills** in a realistic software project environment.

The platform covers key business workflows involving:

* Customers
* Restaurants
* Delivery Partners
* Administrators
* Payment Gateway

The project focuses on understanding business needs, converting them into clear requirements, managing them through Agile practices, and maintaining end-to-end traceability.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customers need a convenient way to discover restaurants, browse food items, place orders, make payments, and track deliveries.

Restaurants need tools to manage their profiles, menus, availability, and customer orders.

Delivery partners need a structured workflow to receive assignments, update delivery status, and complete deliveries.

Administrators need visibility and control over users, restaurants, orders, delivery operations, complaints, and platform-level activities.

Cravio was designed as a case study to analyze these business needs and translate them into structured software requirements.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze business and functional requirements.
* Document requirements using standard BA documentation.
* Visualize business processes and system interactions.
* Convert requirements into Agile user stories and tasks.
* Manage the product backlog and sprint workflow using Jira.
* Establish requirement-to-testing traceability.
* Prepare test cases and UAT scenarios.
* Organize BA deliverables in a professional portfolio structure.

## 📊 Jira Agile Implementation

The Cravio project was structured using **Jira Scrum**.

### Epics

1. Customer Management
2. Restaurant Management
3. Food Ordering
4. Payment Management
5. Delivery Management
6. Admin Management

### Work Items

* **6 Epics**
* **40 User Stories**
* **13 Tasks**

The user stories include acceptance criteria and are organized under their respective business areas.

Two simulated sprints were used to demonstrate backlog management and Agile workflow:

* **Sprint 1 – Customer & Restaurant Foundation**
* **Sprint 2 – Food Ordering & Payment**
